Choose Your River

Five stretches of Utah water, from a two-hour splash on the Weber to six days of red rock wilderness on the Green. Every trip below includes guides, rafts, paddles, helmets, life jackets, and food on the water. Not sure which one fits your group? Tell us who is coming and we will point you to the right river.

Ask Us Which Trip Fits

Cataract Canyon

The biggest whitewater in Utah, and the reason most of our guides moved here. Below the confluence of the Colorado and the Green, the river stacks up into twenty-eight named rapids, ending with the Big Drops — three back-to-back Class IV-V monsters that run at their wildest during the late May runoff.

Four days on the water, three nights camped on sandbars under canyon walls a half mile tall. We handle the kitchen, the tents, and the groceries; you handle the paddle. The trip ends with a float across Lake Powell and a bus ride back to Moab.

  • Length: 4 days, 3 nights
  • Rapids: Class III–V
  • Season: May–September
  • Minimum age: 12

Westwater Canyon

Seventeen miles, eleven rapids, and the darkest rock in the state. Westwater cuts through billion-year-old black schist into a narrow inner gorge, and the whitewater comes fast: Funnel Falls, Sock-It-To-Me, and the infamous Skull Rapid with its Room of Doom eddy waiting on river right.

This is our best single day of big water. Groups meet at dawn in Moab, run the canyon with a riverside lunch on the sand, and are back in town for dinner. Previous rafting experience is not required, but you will get wet and you will be paddling.

  • Length: 1 full day
  • Rapids: Class III–IV
  • Season: April–September
  • Minimum age: 12

Desolation Canyon

Eighty-four miles of Green River through a canyon deeper than the Grand. Sixty friendly rapids keep the days interesting without ever getting frightening, which makes Deso our favorite trip for families and for anyone who wants wilderness more than adrenaline.

Six days of side canyon hikes, Fremont rock art panels, outlaw cabins from the Wild Bunch era, and beaches wide enough for a volleyball game. We bring inflatable kayaks so confident paddlers can run rapids on their own between raft miles.

  • Length: 6 days, 5 nights
  • Rapids: Class II–III
  • Season: May–August
  • Minimum age: 5
